10 分鐘。這份不計對錯,只看有沒有交。請照你真實的想法作答—— 選錯的選項會告訴老師你卡在哪裡,老師才知道要補什麼。

  1. Let $A$ be a $3 \times 5$ matrix and let $B$ be a $5 \times 2$ matrix. Which statement is correct?

    1. A$AB$ is $3 \times 2$, and $BA$ is not defined.
    2. B$AB$ is $5 \times 5$, and $BA$ is $2 \times 3$.
    3. CBoth $AB$ and $BA$ are defined, and $AB = BA$.
    4. D$AB$ is not defined, because $A$ and $B$ have different sizes.

  2. Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & -5 \end{bmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 1 & -2 \end{bmatrix}$. What is the entry in row 2, column 1 of $AB$?

    1. A$-1$
    2. B$8$
    3. C$11$
    4. D$4$

  3. Which formula is correct for all matrices $A$ and $B$ of appropriate sizes?

    1. A$(AB)^T = B^TA^T$
    2. B$(AB)^T = A^TB^T$
    3. C$(A + B)^T = B^T - A^T$
    4. D$(A^T)^T = A^T$

  4. For which value of $k$ is the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 6 \\ 2 & k \end{bmatrix}$ not invertible?

    1. A$k = 4$
    2. B$k = 0$
    3. C$k = 9$
    4. DIt is invertible for every $k$.

  5. To rotate a figure about the origin by $\varphi$ and then translate it by $(h, k)$, which product of homogeneous-coordinate matrices is correct? Write $R$ for the rotation matrix and $T$ for the translation matrix.

    1. A$TR$
    2. B$RT$
    3. C$T + R$
    4. DEither order gives the same result.
