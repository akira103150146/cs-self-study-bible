  1. A system of two linear equations in $x_1, x_2$ describes two parallel lines that do not coincide. How many solutions does the system have?

    1. A$0$
    2. B$1$
    3. C$2$
    4. Dinfinitely many

  2. Which of the following is not an elementary row operation?

    1. ASwap row 1 and row 3.
    2. BMultiply row 2 by $-\frac12$.
    3. CReplace row 3 by (row 3) $- 4 \times$ (row 1).
    4. DMultiply row 1 by $0$.

  3. Which matrix is in reduced echelon form?

    1. A$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
    2. B$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$
    3. C$\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$
    4. D$\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$

  4. The reduced echelon form of an augmented matrix is $\left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 1 & 0 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right]$. How many solutions does the system have?

    1. Anone
    2. Bexactly one
    3. Cinfinitely many
    4. Dcannot tell without the original matrix

  5. For which value of $h$ is the system with augmented matrix $\left[\begin{array}{rr|r} 1 & 2 & h \\ 3 & 6 & 9 \end{array}\right]$ consistent?
