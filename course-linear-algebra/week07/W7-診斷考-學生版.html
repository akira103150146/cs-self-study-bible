10 分鐘。這份不計對錯,只看有沒有交。請照你真實的想法作答—— 選錯的選項會告訴老師你卡在哪裡,老師才知道要補什麼。

  1. Which of the following sets is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$?

    1. AThe set of all $(x, y)$ with $y = 2x$
    2. BThe set of all $(x, y)$ with $y = 2x + 1$
    3. CThe set of all $(x, y)$ with $x \ge 0$ and $y \ge 0$
    4. DThe set of all $(x, y)$ with $xy = 0$

  2. Let $A$ be a $3 \times 5$ matrix. Which statement is correct?

    1. A$\operatorname{Nul} A$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^5$ and $\operatorname{Col} A$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$.
    2. B$\operatorname{Nul} A$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and $\operatorname{Col} A$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^5$.
    3. CBoth $\operatorname{Nul} A$ and $\operatorname{Col} A$ are subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^5$.
    4. D$\operatorname{Col} A = \mathbb{R}^3$, because $A$ has more columns than rows.

  3. The matrix $A$ and an echelon form of $A$ are

    $$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 6 & 5 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

    Which set is a basis for $\operatorname{Col} A$?

    1. A$\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$
    2. B$\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$
    3. C$\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$
    4. D$\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$

  4. Let $\mathbf{b}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$, $\mathbf{b}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}$, $\mathcal{B} = \{\mathbf{b}_1, \mathbf{b}_2\}$, and $[\,\mathbf{x}\,]_{\mathcal{B}} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$. What is $\mathbf{x}$?

    1. A$\begin{bmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$
    2. B$\begin{bmatrix} 7/3 \\ 1/3 \end{bmatrix}$
    3. C$\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$
    4. D$\begin{bmatrix} 7 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}$

  5. Let $A$ be a $4 \times 9$ matrix with $\operatorname{rank} A = 3$. What is $\dim \operatorname{Nul} A$?

    1. A6
    2. B1
    3. C3
    4. D5
