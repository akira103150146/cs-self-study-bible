10 分鐘。這份不計對錯,只看有沒有交。請照你真實的想法作答—— 選錯的選項會告訴老師你卡在哪裡,老師才知道要補什麼。

  1. Let $A$ be a $4 \times 7$ matrix and define $T(\mathbf{x}) = A\mathbf{x}$. What are the domain and the codomain of $T$?

    1. Adomain $\mathbb{R}^7$, codomain $\mathbb{R}^4$
    2. Bdomain $\mathbb{R}^4$, codomain $\mathbb{R}^7$
    3. Cdomain $\mathbb{R}^7$, codomain $\mathbb{R}^7$
    4. DCannot tell without knowing the range of $T$.

  2. Which of the following transformations is not linear?

    1. A$T(x_1, x_2) = (x_1 + x_2,\ x_1 - x_2)$
    2. B$T(\mathbf{x}) = 3\mathbf{x}$
    3. C$T(x_1, x_2) = (x_1 + 1,\ x_2)$
    4. D$T(x_1, x_2) = (0,\ 0)$

  3. A linear transformation $T : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ satisfies $T(\mathbf{e}_1) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $T(\mathbf{e}_2) = \begin{bmatrix} -3 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$. What is the standard matrix of $T$?

    1. A$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 2 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$
    2. B$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -3 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$
    3. C$\begin{bmatrix} -3 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$
    4. D$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$

  4. What does the transformation with standard matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$ do to the plane?

    1. AIt reflects points through the $x_1$-axis.
    2. BIt reflects points through the $x_2$-axis.
    3. CIt rotates points through $90°$.
    4. DIt projects points onto the $x_1$-axis.

  5. Let $A$ be the $5 \times 3$ standard matrix of a linear transformation $T$, and suppose $A$ has 3 pivot positions. Which is true?

    1. A$T$ is one-to-one but not onto $\mathbb{R}^5$.
    2. B$T$ is onto $\mathbb{R}^5$ but not one-to-one.
    3. C$T$ is both one-to-one and onto $\mathbb{R}^5$.
    4. D$T$ is neither one-to-one nor onto $\mathbb{R}^5$.
