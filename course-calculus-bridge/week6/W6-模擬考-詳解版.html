Problem 1 · Limits & Continuity

對應第 1 週 · 20 pts
Problem 1(a)4 pts

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 2}\frac{x^2+x-6}{x^2-4}$.

Solution · 4 pts

代入得 $0/0$,分子分母因式分解:$\dfrac{(x+3)(x-2)}{(x+2)(x-2)}$,約掉共同因式 $(x-2)$ 得 $\dfrac{x+3}{x+2}$,再代 $x=2$。

$$\lim_{x\to 2}\frac{x^2+x-6}{x^2-4}=\frac{2+3}{2+2}=\frac54.$$
Problem 1(b)4 pts

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -\infty}\frac{\sqrt{9x^2+1}}{2x-3}$.

Solution · 4 pts

因 $x\to-\infty$ 故 $x<0$,關鍵是 $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|=-x$。$\sqrt{9x^2+1}=|x|\sqrt{9+\tfrac1{x^2}}=-x\sqrt{9+\tfrac1{x^2}}$,上下同除 $x$:

$$\frac{-\sqrt{9+\frac1{x^2}}}{2-\frac3x}\longrightarrow\frac{-3}{2}.$$

少補這個負號答案就會變成 $+\tfrac32$,整題翻盤。

Problem 1(c)4 pts

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin 5x}{\sin 3x}$.

Solution · 4 pts

湊招牌極限 $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta}\to1$:

$$\frac{\sin 5x}{\sin 3x}=\frac{\sin 5x}{5x}\cdot\frac{3x}{\sin 3x}\cdot\frac{5}{3}\longrightarrow 1\cdot 1\cdot\frac53=\frac53.$$
Problem 1(d)4 pts

Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}, & x<1\\[6pt] ax+3, & x\ge 1\end{cases}$. Find the value of $a$ that makes $f$ continuous at $x=1$.

Solution · 4 pts

左段極限 $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to1^-}\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}=\lim_{x\to1^-}(x+1)=2$;右段(含端點)$f(1)=a+3$。連續要求接縫兩邊相等:

$$a+3=2\;\Rightarrow\; a=-1.$$
Problem 1(e)4 pts

Use the Intermediate Value Theorem to show that $x^3-3x+1=0$ has a root in the interval $(0,1)$.

Solution · 4 pts

令 $f(x)=x^3-3x+1$,為多項式,在 $[0,1]$ 上連續(先寫這句)。計算兩端:

$$f(0)=1>0,\qquad f(1)=1-3+1=-1<0.$$

兩端異號,由中間值定理存在 $c\in(0,1)$ 使 $f(c)=0$。注意結論只保證「根存在」,不給出根的值。

Problem 2 · Derivatives & Differentiation Rules

對應第 2 週 · 20 pts
Problem 2(a)4 pts

Using the limit definition of the derivative, find $f'(x)$ for $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$.

Solution · 4 pts

由定義並對分子乘共軛 $\sqrt{x+h}+\sqrt{x}$:

$$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sqrt{x+h}-\sqrt{x}}{h}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{(x+h)-x}{h\left(\sqrt{x+h}+\sqrt{x}\right)}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+h}+\sqrt{x}}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}.$$
Problem 2(b)4 pts

Differentiate $y=\dfrac{x^2+1}{x-1}$.

Solution · 4 pts

商法則 $\left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)'=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$,$u=x^2+1$、$v=x-1$:

$$y'=\frac{(2x)(x-1)-(x^2+1)(1)}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{2x^2-2x-x^2-1}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{x^2-2x-1}{(x-1)^2}.$$
Problem 2(c)4 pts

Differentiate $y=\sqrt{x^2+4x}$.

Solution · 4 pts

鏈鎖法則:外層 $\sqrt{\;}$ 微分 $\times$ 內層微分。

$$y'=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x^2+4x}}\cdot(2x+4)=\frac{x+2}{\sqrt{x^2+4x}}.$$
Problem 2(d)4 pts

Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ by implicit differentiation: $x^3+y^3=6xy$.

Solution · 4 pts

兩邊對 $x$ 微分,$y$ 是 $x$ 的函數(右邊用乘積法則):

$$3x^2+3y^2\,y'=6y+6x\,y'.$$

把含 $y'$ 的項移到一邊:$y'(3y^2-6x)=6y-3x^2$,故

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{6y-3x^2}{3y^2-6x}=\frac{2y-x^2}{y^2-2x}.$$
Problem 2(e)4 pts

Use logarithmic differentiation to find $y'$ for $y=x^{\sin x}$ (with $x>0$).

Solution · 4 pts

底數與指數都含 $x$,兩邊取自然對數:$\ln y=\sin x\cdot\ln x$。再對 $x$ 微分(左邊隱函數、右邊乘積法則):

$$\frac{y'}{y}=\cos x\,\ln x+\sin x\cdot\frac1x.$$

乘回 $y=x^{\sin x}$:

$$y'=x^{\sin x}\!\left(\cos x\,\ln x+\frac{\sin x}{x}\right).$$

Problem 3 · Related Rates, Linear Approximation & L'Hôpital

對應第 3 週 · 16 pts
Problem 3(a)6 pts

Evaluate the following limits (L'Hôpital's Rule is permitted).
  (i) $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}$   [3]
  (ii) $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\ln x}{\sqrt{x}}$   [3]

Solution · 6 pts

(i) 代入是 $\dfrac00$ 不定型,用 L'Hôpital。第一次微分仍是 $\dfrac00$,再用一次:

$$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{2x}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x}{2}=\frac12.$$

(ii) 型態 $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$,用一次 L'Hôpital:

$$\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\ln x}{\sqrt{x}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1/x}{1/(2\sqrt{x})}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2\sqrt{x}}{x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}=0.$$

(直覺:$\ln x$ 長得比任何正冪次都慢。)

Problem 3(b)5 pts

Use a linear approximation (differentials) to estimate $\sqrt{4.1}$. Give a decimal answer.

Solution · 5 pts

令 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$,選好算的基準點 $a=4$。$f(4)=2$,$f'(x)=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$,$f'(4)=\dfrac14$。線性近似 $L(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$:

$$\sqrt{4.1}\approx L(4.1)=2+\frac14(4.1-4)=2+\frac14(0.1)=2.025.$$

(真值 $\approx 2.0248$,誤差約 $0.0002$;$dy=f'(a)\,dx$ 就是微分量。)

Problem 3(c)5 pts

A spherical balloon is being inflated at a rate of $100\ \mathrm{cm^3/s}$. How fast is the radius increasing at the instant the radius is $5\ \mathrm{cm}$? (Volume $V=\tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.)

Solution · 5 pts

相關變率:對時間 $t$ 微分 $V=\tfrac43\pi r^3$,鏈鎖法則得

$$\frac{dV}{dt}=4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}.$$

代入已知 $\dfrac{dV}{dt}=100$、$r=5$:$100=4\pi(5)^2\dfrac{dr}{dt}=100\pi\dfrac{dr}{dt}$,故

$$\frac{dr}{dt}=\frac{1}{\pi}\approx 0.318\ \mathrm{cm/s}.$$

Problem 4 · Extrema, MVT & Curve Sketching

對應第 4 週 · 16 pts
Problem 4(a)4 pts

Verify that $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ satisfies the hypotheses of the Mean Value Theorem on $[0,4]$, then find all values $c$ that satisfy its conclusion.

Solution · 4 pts

$f$ 在 $[0,4]$ 連續、在 $(0,4)$ 可微,MVT 前提成立。割線斜率

$$\frac{f(4)-f(0)}{4-0}=\frac{2-0}{4}=\frac12.$$

令 $f'(c)=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{c}}=\dfrac12$,解得 $\sqrt{c}=1$,即 $c=1\in(0,4)$。

Problem 4(b)12 pts

Consider $f(x)=x^3-3x^2$.
  (i) Find the intervals on which $f$ is increasing / decreasing. [3]
  (ii) Find all local maxima and minima. [3]
  (iii) Find the intervals of concavity and any inflection point(s). [3]
  (iv) Using the information above, sketch the graph of $f$. [3]

Solution · 12 pts

(i) $f'(x)=3x^2-6x=3x(x-2)$,臨界點 $x=0,2$。$f'>0$ 於 $(-\infty,0)$ 與 $(2,\infty)$(遞增);$f'<0$ 於 $(0,2)$(遞減)。

(ii) 一階判別:$x=0$ 由增轉減 → 局部極大 $f(0)=0$;$x=2$ 由減轉增 → 局部極小 $f(2)=8-12=-4$。

(iii) $f''(x)=6x-6=6(x-1)$,零點 $x=1$。$f''<0$ 於 $x<1$(凹向下),$f''>0$ 於 $x>1$(凹向上),反曲點 $(1,-2)$。

(iv) 圖形:$f(x)=x^2(x-3)$,與 $x$ 軸交於 $x=0$(重根,相切)與 $x=3$。左方遞增升到極大 $(0,0)$,遞減穿過反曲點 $(1,-2)$ 抵極小 $(2,-4)$,之後遞增並在 $x=3$ 穿過 $x$ 軸往上。

Problem 5 · Optimization

對應第 4 週 · 12 pts
Problem 512 pts

A farmer has $400$ meters of fencing and wants to enclose a rectangular field bordering a straight river. No fence is needed along the river side. Find the dimensions of the field that maximize the enclosed area, and state the maximum area.

Solution · 12 pts

設變數。設垂直河岸的兩邊各長 $w$、平行河岸的一邊長 $\ell$。只有三邊需要柵欄:$2w+\ell=400$,故 $\ell=400-2w$,其中 $0<w<200$。 [設定 4 分]

目標函數。面積

$$A(w)=w\ell=w(400-2w)=400w-2w^2.$$

求極值。$A'(w)=400-4w=0\Rightarrow w=100$;$A''(w)=-4<0$,確認為極大。 [微分 4 分]

作答。$\ell=400-2(100)=200$。最大面積

$$A=100\times200=20000\ \mathrm{m^2}.$$

尺寸:兩邊各 $100\ \mathrm{m}$、平行河岸一邊 $200\ \mathrm{m}$。 [作答 4 分]

Problem 6 · Integration & the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

對應第 5 週 · 16 pts
Problem 6(a)4 pts

Evaluate the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int\left(4x^3-6x+\sec^2 x\right)dx$.

Solution · 4 pts

逐項反導:$\int 4x^3\,dx=x^4$,$\int(-6x)\,dx=-3x^2$,$\int\sec^2 x\,dx=\tan x$。

$$\int\left(4x^3-6x+\sec^2 x\right)dx=x^4-3x^2+\tan x+C.$$

不定積分別忘記 $+C$。

Problem 6(b)4 pts

Evaluate the definite integral $\displaystyle\int_1^2\left(x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}\right)dx$ using the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

Solution · 4 pts

先求反導 $F(x)=\dfrac{x^3}{3}-\dfrac1x$,再由 FTC 代上下限相減:

$$\left[\frac{x^3}{3}-\frac1x\right]_1^2=\left(\frac83-\frac12\right)-\left(\frac13-1\right)=\frac{13}{6}-\left(-\frac46\right)=\frac{17}{6}.$$
Problem 6(c)4 pts

Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x\sqrt{1+x^2}\,dx$ using a suitable substitution.

Solution · 4 pts

換元 $u=1+x^2$,則 $du=2x\,dx$,即 $x\,dx=\tfrac12\,du$;上下限一併換:$x:0\to1$ 對應 $u:1\to2$。

$$\int_0^1 x\sqrt{1+x^2}\,dx=\int_1^2\frac12\sqrt{u}\,du=\frac12\cdot\frac{2}{3}u^{3/2}\Big|_1^2=\frac13\left(2^{3/2}-1\right)=\frac{2\sqrt2-1}{3}\approx0.609.$$
Problem 6(d)4 pts

Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\displaystyle\int_1^{x^2}\cos\!\left(t^2\right)dt$.

Solution · 4 pts

FTC 第一部分搭配鏈鎖法則:上限是 $u=x^2$,把被積函數在上限值代入,再乘上限的導數。

$$\frac{d}{dx}\int_1^{x^2}\cos(t^2)\,dt=\cos\!\left((x^2)^2\right)\cdot\frac{d}{dx}(x^2)=2x\cos\!\left(x^4\right).$$
