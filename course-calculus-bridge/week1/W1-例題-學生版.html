一起想:先講「連續」,再算兩端符號。結論只能說什麼?

作答區
換你練習
  1. Show that $x^3-6x+2=0$ has a root in $(0,1)$.
  2. Show that $e^x=2-x$ has a solution in $(0,1)$.
  3. Let $f$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ with $0\le f(x)\le1$. Show that there exists $c$ with $f(c)=c$ (a fixed point).
