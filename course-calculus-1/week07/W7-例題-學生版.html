When the Techniques Run Out｜四招都失效時

Some integrals have no elementary antiderivative — that is a theorem, not a skill gap
🧭
引導推導

跟著老師一步步想,自己把道理推出來:

  1. $\displaystyle\int e^{-x^{2}}dx$ 你會用哪一招?換元?分部?三角代換?部分分式?
  2. 四招都試過都不行。這表示你不夠強,還是這題本來就沒有初等原函數?
  3. $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}e^{-x^{2}}dx$ 這個數字存在嗎?(想:被積函數連續嗎?黎曼和收斂嗎?)
  4. 如果你手上只有一串感測器讀數,沒有函數公式,還能算積分嗎?
一起做 · 示範
示範基礎

Explain why $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}e^{-x^{2}}dx$ is a well-defined number even though $e^{-x^{2}}$ has no elementary antiderivative.

一起想:分開想兩件事:①這個數存不存在 ②能不能用公式表示。

作答區
換你練習
  1. Which of these have elementary antiderivatives? (i) $xe^{-x^{2}}$   (ii) $e^{-x^{2}}$   (iii) $\dfrac{\sin x}{x}$
  2. Give a situation in computing where you must integrate but have no formula at all.
  3. Is $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\sin x}{x}dx$ well-defined at $x=0$?
