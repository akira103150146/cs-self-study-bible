What Is a Differential Equation｜什麼是微分方程

An equation whose unknown is a function, not a number
🧭
引導推導

跟著老師一步步想,自己把道理推出來:

  1. $x^{2}-4=0$ 的未知數是什麼?$y'=2y$ 的未知數是什麼?
  2. 驗證 $y=e^{2t}$ 滿足 $y'=2y$。那 $y=5e^{2t}$ 呢?$y=Ce^{2t}$ 呢?
  3. 所以微分方程的解有幾個?為什麼會有任意常數?
  4. 要唯一決定一個解,還需要什麼?
一起做 · 示範
示範基礎

Show that $y=Ce^{2t}$ solves $y'=2y$ for every constant $C$, and find the particular solution with $y(0)=3$.

一起想:把 $y$ 微分一次,和右邊比對。初始條件代 $t=0$。

作答區
換你練習
  1. Verify that $y=\sin t$ solves $y''+y=0$.
  2. Find the particular solution of $y'=3y$ with $y(0)=5$.
  3. Why does the general solution of a first-order equation contain exactly one arbitrary constant?
