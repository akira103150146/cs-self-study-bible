Problem 1 · Area and Volume

對應第 10 週 · 16 pts
Problem 1(a)5 pts

Find the area of the region enclosed by $y=x^{2}$ and $y=2x$.

Solution · 5 pts

交點:$x^{2}=2x\Rightarrow x=0,2$。在 $(0,2)$ 上 $2x>x^{2}$。[求交點並判斷上下 2 分]

$$A=\int_{0}^{2}\left(2x-x^{2}\right)dx=\left[x^{2}-\frac{x^{3}}{3}\right]_{0}^{2}=4-\frac83=\frac43.$$[積分 3 分]
Problem 1(b)5 pts

The region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.

Solution · 5 pts

圓盤法(繞 $x$ 軸、函數已是 $y=f(x)$,不必反解):

$$V=\int_{0}^{4}\pi\left(\sqrt x\right)^{2}dx=\pi\int_{0}^{4}x\,dx=\pi\left[\frac{x^{2}}{2}\right]_{0}^{4}=8\pi.$$[選對方法 2 分、記得平方 1 分、積分 2 分]
Problem 1(c)6 pts

The region under $y=x^{2}$ on $[0,2]$ is rotated about the $y$-axis. Find the volume, and state which method you used and why.

Solution · 6 pts

殼層法——繞 $y$ 軸但函數是 $y=f(x)$,用圓盤要先反解 $x=\sqrt y$。[說出理由 2 分]

$$V=\int_{0}^{2}2\pi x\cdot x^{2}dx=2\pi\int_{0}^{2}x^{3}dx=2\pi\left[\frac{x^{4}}{4}\right]_{0}^{2}=8\pi.$$[列式 2 分、計算 2 分。漏掉半徑 $x$ 者扣 2 分]

Problem 2 · Arc Length and Surface Area

對應第 11 週 · 14 pts
Problem 2(a)6 pts

Find the arc length of $y=\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}$ on $[0,3]$.

Solution · 6 pts

$f'=\sqrt x$,故 $\sqrt{1+\left(f'\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{1+x}$:

$$L=\int_{0}^{3}\sqrt{1+x}\,dx=\left[\frac23(1+x)^{3/2}\right]_{0}^{3}=\frac23(8-1)=\frac{14}{3}.$$[被積式 3 分(裡面是導數的平方)、積分 3 分]
Problem 2(b)8 pts

The line $y=x$ on $[0,2]$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the surface area, and explain why the width of each band is not $dx$.

Solution · 8 pts

$f'=1$,斜長因子 $\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2$:

$$S=\int_{0}^{2}2\pi x\cdot\sqrt2\,dx=2\sqrt2\,\pi\left[\frac{x^{2}}{2}\right]_{0}^{2}=4\sqrt2\,\pi.$$[被積式含斜長 3 分、積分 2 分]

為什麼不是 $dx$:曲面是沿著曲線展開的,不是沿 $x$ 軸的投影。曲線越陡,同樣的 $dx$ 對應越寬的曲面帶。用 $dx$ 會系統性低估(這裡會少 $\sqrt2$ 倍)。[說明 3 分]

(驗算:圓錐側面積 $\pi r\ell=\pi\cdot2\cdot2\sqrt2=4\sqrt2\pi$ ✓)

Problem 3 · Averages and Expected Value

對應第 12 週 · 16 pts
Problem 3(a)4 pts

Find the average value of $f(x)=x^{3}$ on $[0,2]$.

Solution · 4 pts$$f_{\text{avg}}=\frac{1}{2-0}\int_{0}^{2}x^{3}dx=\frac12\left[\frac{x^{4}}{4}\right]_{0}^{2}=\frac12\cdot4=2.$$[除以區間長度 2 分、積分 2 分]
Problem 3(b)5 pts

Find the constant $c$ that makes $p(x)=ce^{-3x}$ a probability density on $[0,\infty)$.

Solution · 5 pts

$\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-3x}dx=\lim_{b\to\infty}\left[-\frac{e^{-3x}}{3}\right]_{0}^{b}=\frac13$。[瑕積分寫成極限 3 分]

要總積分為 1:$\dfrac{c}{3}=1\Rightarrow c=3$。[求 $c$ 2 分]

Problem 3(c)7 pts

For the density $p(x)=3e^{-3x}$ on $[0,\infty)$, compute $\mathbb{E}[X]$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)$.

Solution · 7 pts

期望值(分部積分):

$$\mathbb{E}[X]=\int_{0}^{\infty}3xe^{-3x}dx=\frac13.$$[3 分]

二階矩(再分部一次):

$$\mathbb{E}\left[X^{2}\right]=\int_{0}^{\infty}3x^{2}e^{-3x}dx=\frac{2}{9}.$$[2 分]

變異數(用捷徑公式):

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}\left[X^{2}\right]-\mu^{2}=\frac29-\frac19=\frac19.$$[2 分。寫成 $\mu^{2}-\mathbb{E}[X^{2}]$ 者 0 分]

(指數分佈的 $\mathbb{E}[X]=\frac1\lambda$、$\operatorname{Var}=\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}$,此處 $\lambda=3$ ✓)

Problem 4 · Parametric Curves and Polar Coordinates

對應第 13 週 · 16 pts
Problem 4(a)4 pts

For $x=t^{2}$, $y=t^{3}$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ and the points where the tangent is vertical.

Solution · 4 pts$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}=\frac{3t^{2}}{2t}=\frac{3t}{2}\quad(t\ne0).$$[2 分]

鉛直切線需 $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=2t=0$ 且 $\dfrac{dy}{dt}\ne0$。但 $t=0$ 時 $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=3t^{2}=0$ 也為零——這是尖點,不是鉛直切線。[2 分。答「$t=0$ 有鉛直切線」者只給 1 分]

Problem 4(b)6 pts

Find the area enclosed by the polar curve $r=3\cos\theta$. State your range of $\theta$ and why.

Solution · 6 pts

$r=3\cos\theta$ 是通過原點、直徑 $3$ 的圓。$\theta\in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 就畫完一整圈(超過 $r$ 會變負、重畫一遍)。[範圍與理由 3 分]

$$A=\frac12\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}9\cos^{2}\theta\,d\theta=\frac92\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{9\pi}{4}.$$[計算 3 分。取 $[0,2\pi]$ 得 $\frac{9\pi}{2}$ 者扣 3 分]

(驗算:半徑 $\frac32$ 的圓面積 $=\pi\left(\frac32\right)^{2}=\frac{9\pi}{4}$ ✓)

Problem 4(c)6 pts

Find the arc length of $x=\cos t$, $y=\sin t$ for $t\in[0,\pi]$, and explain what the integrand represents physically.

Solution · 6 pts$$\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}=\sin^{2}t+\cos^{2}t=1\ \Longrightarrow\ L=\int_{0}^{\pi}1\,dt=\pi.$$[3 分。這是單位圓的半周長 ✓]

物理意義:$\sqrt{\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}}$ 是速率(速度向量的長度)。弧長 = 速率對時間積分 = 總路程。此處速率恆為 1,走了 $\pi$ 個時間單位,故路程 $\pi$。[3 分]

Problem 5 · Differential Equations

對應第 14–15 週 · 22 pts
Problem 5(a)5 pts

Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=2ty$ with $y(0)=3$.

Solution · 5 pts

可分離:$\dfrac{dy}{y}=2t\,dt$(暫設 $y\ne0$)。積分:

$$\ln|y|=t^{2}+C_{1}\ \Longrightarrow\ y=Ce^{t^{2}}.$$[分離與積分 3 分]

$y(0)=C=3$,故 $y=3e^{t^{2}}$。[初始條件 2 分]

(平衡解 $y\equiv0$ 對應 $C=0$,已含在通解中。)

Problem 5(b)7 pts

Solve $y'+2y=e^{t}$ with $y(0)=0$ using an integrating factor.

Solution · 7 pts

積分因子:$p=2$,故 $\mu=e^{2t}$。[2 分]

兩邊乘 $\mu$,左邊成為 $\left(e^{2t}y\right)'$:

$$\left(e^{2t}y\right)'=e^{2t}e^{t}=e^{3t}\ \Longrightarrow\ e^{2t}y=\frac{e^{3t}}{3}+C.$$[3 分]

除以 $\mu$($C$ 也要除):$y=\dfrac{e^{t}}{3}+Ce^{-2t}$。

$y(0)=\dfrac13+C=0\Rightarrow C=-\dfrac13$,故

$$y=\frac{e^{t}-e^{-2t}}{3}.$$[2 分。$C$ 沒除者扣 2 分]
Problem 5(c)4 pts

A radioactive substance has a half-life of $8$ days. How long until only $\tfrac18$ of the original amount remains?

Solution · 4 pts

$\dfrac18=\left(\dfrac12\right)^{3}$——恰好 3 個半衰期:

$$t=3\times8=24\ \text{days}.$$[認出是 3 個半衰期 3 分、答案 1 分]

(硬算也可:$k=\frac{\ln2}{8}$,解 $e^{-kt}=\frac18$ 得 $t=\frac{8\ln8}{\ln2}=24$。)

Problem 5(d)6 pts

Apply Euler's method with $h=0.5$ to $y'=y-t$, $y(0)=1$, for two steps. Then state the order of the global error and justify it in one sentence.

Solution · 6 pts

迭代 $y_{n+1}=y_{n}+h\left(y_{n}-t_{n}\right)$:

$y_{1}=1+0.5(1-0)=1.5$($t=0.5$)

$y_{2}=1.5+0.5(1.5-0.5)=2.0$($t=1$)

[每步 2 分,共 4 分]

全域誤差 $O(h)$:單步誤差是 $O(h^{2})$(泰勒的二階項),但走到固定時刻需要 $n=\frac{T}{h}$ 步,$\frac{T}{h}\times O(h^{2})=O(h)$——累積掉一階。[2 分]

Problem 6 · Gradient Flow and Stability

對應第 16–17 週 · 16 pts
Problem 6(a)6 pts

For $L(\theta)=\tfrac14\theta^{4}-\tfrac12\theta^{2}$, write the gradient flow, find its equilibria, and classify each as stable or unstable.

Solution · 6 pts

梯度流:$L'=\theta^{3}-\theta$,故

$$\theta'=-L'(\theta)=-\theta^{3}+\theta=-\theta(\theta-1)(\theta+1).$$[2 分。忘記負號者扣 2 分]

平衡點:$\theta=0,\pm1$。[1 分]

分類(用 $L''=3\theta^{2}-1$,穩定 $\iff L''>0$):

$\theta^{*}$$L''$分類
$-1$$2>0$穩定(局部極小)
$0$$-1<0$不穩定(局部極大)
$1$$2>0$穩定(局部極小)
[3 分]

(所以初始值的正負決定收斂到哪個極小——這就是吸引域。)

Problem 6(b)5 pts

For $L(\theta)=\tfrac{10}{2}\theta^{2}$, gradient descent uses $\theta\leftarrow\theta-\eta L'(\theta)$. Find the largest stable $\eta$ and the $\eta$ that converges fastest.

Solution · 5 pts

$L'=10\theta$,故迭代為

$$\theta_{n+1}=\theta_{n}-10\eta\,\theta_{n}=\left(1-10\eta\right)\theta_{n}.$$[寫出公比 2 分]

穩定需 $\left|1-10\eta\right|<1$,即

$$0<\eta<\frac{2}{10}=0.2.$$[2 分]

最快:公比為 $0$ 時一步到位,即 $\eta=\dfrac{1}{10}=0.1=\dfrac{1}{L''}$——那正是牛頓法。[1 分]

Problem 6(c)5 pts

Explain in three sentences why gradient descent is Euler's method (not merely analogous), and what $\eta$ corresponds to.

Solution · 5 pts

參考答案(三句,要點齊全即給滿分):

① 梯度流是自治方程 $\theta'=-\nabla L(\theta)$,描述「連續地往下坡走」。[2 分]

② 把 Euler 法 $y_{n+1}=y_{n}+hf(y_{n})$ 套上去,得 $\theta_{n+1}=\theta_{n}-h\nabla L(\theta_{n})$,與梯度下降的更新式完全相同(不只形式相似,是同一個式子)。[2 分]

③ 因此 learning rate $\eta$ 就是步長 $h$;$\eta$ 太大會違反 Euler 的穩定條件而發散,上限由曲率決定($\eta<\frac{2}{L''}$)。[1 分]

批改提醒:只寫「很像」或「都是迭代」不給分;必須指出兩個式子相同,以及 $\eta=h$。
